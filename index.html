Mode Bridge.

A Mode Bridge moves ETH and supported tokens between Ethereum and Mode Network, an OP Stack layer-2 inside the Optimism Superchain that shares a cut of sequencer revenue with the developers building on it. You choose the amount, see what will land before you sign, and keep custody of your assets at every step — deposits settle on Mode in minutes, while a withdrawal back to Ethereum passes through the standard challenge period before the funds are released.

How the estimate is calculated

Fee = 0.25 units + 0.3% of the amount, rounded to two decimal places and capped at the amount sent. The remaining amount uses the same units, at a 1:1 conversion. Time starts at 2 minutes, adds 1 minute per 2,500 units or part thereof, and is capped at 12 minutes. The displayed range adds 2 minutes to that estimate.

What a Mode Bridge does

A Mode Bridge locks your ETH or token on Ethereum and mints the matching amount on Mode Network, the OP Stack layer-2 built inside the Optimism Superchain. Mode runs its own execution environment and sequencer, but every batch of transactions is posted back to Ethereum as the data that lets the rollup's state be verified from the base layer. A deposit only needs Ethereum to confirm, so the asset appears on Mode within minutes. An exit runs the other direction — the asset is burned on Mode and released on Ethereum once the standard security window has passed — and the amount you will receive, along with the gas it will cost, is shown before you ever sign anything.

Mode's place in the Superchain

Mode shares its rollup software, its fraud-proof design and its settlement path to Ethereum with the rest of the OP Stack chains, a shared architecture that Optimism's own documentation lays out in detail. That means the deposit and withdrawal mechanics work the same way they do on sibling Superchain networks, while Mode's sequencer and bridge contracts handle everything specific to the Mode chain itself.

What you can move across

ETH is the default deposit, and the canonical route also carries the major ERC-20 tokens that circulate on Mode. A token keeps its identity on arrival — the contract standard and ticker don't change, only the chain it lives on — and it can travel back to Ethereum through the same path whenever you choose.

Mode Bridge and the sequencer-fee model Mode Network runs on

Most rollups keep the fees their sequencer collects. Mode splits that revenue instead, sending a share of it to the developers whose contracts generated the activity that produced the fee — an onchain rewards design that Mode's documentation sets out as the chain's core incentive. None of that changes how a deposit or a withdrawal actually works, but it is the reason a growing set of DeFi teams build on Mode specifically rather than on a generic OP Stack deployment: usage on the chain feeds back to the apps that create it, not only to whoever runs the sequencer.

Mode Bridge fees and timing

A deposit costs the Ethereum gas needed to run the transaction, plus Mode's own low execution fee once the balance lands — there is no separate bridge toll beyond gas. Withdrawals cost gas on both ends, once to start the exit on Mode and once to finalize it on Ethereum, and they run far longer than a deposit because the optimistic design holds the funds during a window where the state can still be disputed. Reading the net amount

The figure that matters is what actually lands after gas, not the headline rate a route might advertise. On a small transfer, the fixed Ethereum gas cost can eat a larger share of the total than it would on a bigger one, so check the quoted net amount against your own math before you sign, particularly if you're moving a modest balance.

Deposit and withdrawal flow, step by step

The steps stay the same on every transfer, which is what makes the process predictable after you've done it once.
  • Open the bridge interface and pick Ethereum as the source chain, Mode as the destination.
  • Choose ETH or a supported token and enter the amount; the interface shows the fee and the amount that will land.
  • Sign the deposit transaction in your wallet and pay the Ethereum gas it requires.
  • Wait for Ethereum to confirm — your balance shows up on Mode a few minutes later.
  • To exit, initiate a withdrawal on Mode, let the challenge period pass, then finalize the claim on Ethereum.

Why the exit takes about a week, not minutes

A deposit only needs one Ethereum confirmation. A withdrawal has to sit through the dispute window that protects every optimistic rollup — the same mechanism described generally in the overview of optimistic rollups on Wikipedia — so that a wrong state root can still be challenged before any ETH actually leaves the bridge contract on Ethereum.

Planning around the wait

Anyone who needs funds back on Ethereum against a deadline should start the withdrawal with the full window in mind, or weigh a liquidity-provider bridge that fronts the funds immediately for a fee, a trade-off covered later in this guide.

Security behind a Mode Bridge transfer

Mode inherits its security from Ethereum through fraud proofs rather than from its own validator set, so the trust assumption behind every transfer rests on the base layer and on the dispute mechanism, not on Mode's sequencer operator.

Fraud proofs and the challenge period

During the challenge period, any party can submit proof that a posted state root is wrong and stop a fraudulent withdrawal before it pays out. That mechanism is standard across OP Stack chains and tracked, along with Mode's other risk parameters, on L2BEAT's page for the network.

What you still control

Confirm the contract address of any token before you bridge it, and keep the number of standing approvals small. The signature you review in your wallet before each transaction is the one moment where a mistake actually happens — the bridge contract itself never asks for anything beyond that signature.

What to do once a Mode Bridge deposit lands

Once ETH or a token sits on Mode, it works across the chain's DeFi stack — exchanges, lending markets and the apps whose usage earns them a cut of sequencer revenue under Mode's rewards model. You can confirm a deposit landed by searching your address on the Mode network explorer, and when you're ready to move value back, the same canonical route handles the return trip to Ethereum.

Mode Bridge vs third-party routes

The canonical Mode Bridge is the reference path: it mints the official representation of your asset and is backed directly by the OP Stack's fraud-proof system rather than by a third party's balance sheet. A liquidity bridge can shorten the withdrawal side from about a week to minutes by fronting the funds itself, but it adds its own fee and its own trust assumptions on top of Mode's. For a large or unhurried transfer, the canonical route set out in Mode's documentation is the simpler choice; for a withdrawal that can't wait, weigh a third party's reputation and its quoted net amount against the time it saves.

What is a Mode Bridge?
A Mode Bridge is the official deposit and withdrawal route between Ethereum and Mode Network, the OP Stack layer-2 that settles its state back to Ethereum while running its own execution and sequencer.
How long does a Mode Bridge withdrawal take?
About a week in most cases, because the exit waits through the standard challenge period before funds are released on Ethereum. A deposit, by contrast, lands in minutes.
What is the Optimism Superchain and how does Mode fit into it?
The Superchain is the family of OP Stack chains that share the same rollup software and settle to Ethereum through a common framework. Mode is one of those chains, running its own sequencer and adding its sequencer-fee rewards on top of the shared design.
What are sequencer-fee rewards on Mode?
Mode shares a portion of the revenue its sequencer collects with the developers whose contracts generate the activity behind that revenue, an incentive meant to draw builders to the chain rather than to a generic rollup.
What does a deposit to Mode cost?
Just the Ethereum gas required to run the deposit transaction. Mode itself charges a low execution fee once the balance lands, with no separate bridge toll on top of gas.
Is a Mode Bridge safe to use?
The canonical route relies on Ethereum's security through fraud proofs rather than on Mode's own sequencer, and it never takes custody of your keys. The real risks sit at signing — the contract address you're trusting and the approval you grant — not in the bridge design itself.
Can I bridge tokens other than ETH?
Yes. The canonical route also carries the main ERC-20 tokens that circulate on Mode, and each one keeps its ticker and contract standard on arrival.
